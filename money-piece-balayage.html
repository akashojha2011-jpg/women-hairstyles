15+ Money Piece with Balayage for a Seamless Blend

Cindy Marcus
Senior Hairstylist
| PHOTOS UPDATED ON SEPTEMBER 12, 2026
Reviewed & Fact-Checked by Licensed Master Stylist Elena Vance

Face-framing color has never looked better than when a bold money piece meets the seamless blend of balayage. Together, these two techniques create a hair color look that’s dimensional, face-brightening, and completely wearable for everyday life. Whether you’re a brunette craving a touch of warmth, a blonde wanting more contrast, or someone exploring soft pastels and copper tones, the money piece with balayage combo delivers major impact with surprisingly low maintenance. This pairing works across all hair lengths, textures, and skin tones — making it one of the most versatile color approaches in modern hair. Read on for 20 stunning money piece with balayage looks to bring to your next salon appointment.

1. Ash Blonde Money Piece with Brunette Balayage

Cool, clean, and undeniably chic — this look pairs ash blonde money pieces at the front with soft balayage swept through brunette lengths. The cool-toned framing strips cut through the warmth of the brown base and give the face a fresh, lit-from-within glow. It’s one of those color combinations that looks polished even as it grows out. The contrast between the icy face-framing pieces and the deeper brunette midsections creates beautiful movement and dimension. This works especially well for those with cool or neutral skin undertones. Your colorist will use a freehand painting technique on the body of the hair while keeping the money pieces more defined and direct for a sharp yet seamless finish.

WHY WE LOVE IT
Creates weightless height, quick morning styling time, and high visual volume.
STYLING CONSIDERATIONS
Requires regular maintenance trims every 6-8 weeks to keep shape sharp.
TECHNIQUE USED
Point-cut layers with soft texturizing through mid-lengths.
BEST FACE SHAPE
Oval, Heart, Square, Round
HAIR DENSITY
Fine to Medium Density
