17+ Gorgeous Date Night Hairstyle with Half Up Twist for a Romantic Evening

Cindy Marcus
Senior Hairstylist
| PHOTOS UPDATED ON OCTOBER 15, 2026
Reviewed & Fact-Checked by Licensed Master Stylist Elena Vance

Getting ready for a romantic evening out should be fun, not stressful. If you are looking for the perfect way to style your hair, exploring a 20 date night hairstyle with half up twist options will give you plenty of inspiration. This specific look beautifully combines the elegance of an updo with the soft romance of flowing locks. It works wonderfully for almost any hair texture and length, making it a highly versatile choice for your special night. Whether you prefer something tight and sleek or loose and bohemian, there is a twisted variation that will perfectly frame your face and complement your outfit. Let us dive into some stunning ways you can wear this gorgeous look.

1. Half Up Twist With Loose Curls

Soft, bouncy curls paired with a twisted front section create a remarkably romantic vibe. You can use a curling iron to form loose ringlets, then gently brush them out for that flawless wave. Take two small sections of hair from the front, twist them back, and secure them at the crown with a discreet pin. This leaves the rest of your curls cascading beautifully down your back. It is a timeless choice that looks incredibly effortless yet polished. The twisted front keeps the hair out of your face while you are enjoying your evening, and the loose curls add stunning movement. This style works exceptionally well for medium to long hair.

WHY WE LOVE IT
Effortless face-framing elegance, keeps hair off the face, and showcases length.
STYLING CONSIDERATIONS
Secure with mini clear elastics or texturizing spray to prevent slipping.
TECHNIQUE USED
Crown sectioning with twist, pin, or decorative clip placement.
BEST FACE SHAPE
Universal
HAIR DENSITY
Fine, Medium, and Thick Densities
