13+ Beautiful Curtain Bangs with Layers for Extra Movement and Shape

Cindy Marcus
Senior Hairstylist
| PHOTOS UPDATED ON SEPTEMBER 12, 2026
Reviewed & Fact-Checked by Licensed Master Stylist Elena Vance

Curtain bangs paired with layers have become the ultimate go-to hairstyle for anyone looking to refresh their look without losing length or committing to a high-maintenance cut. This versatile combination offers a soft, face-framing effect that works beautifully across various hair textures and face shapes. Whether you have straight, wavy, or curly hair, adding these sweeping bangs and strategic layers can provide instant volume and movement. Many people love how this style bridges the gap between a classic cut and a modern, trendy aesthetic. It is perfect for those who want an effortless vibe that looks just as good when air-dried as it does when professionally blown out.

1. Long layered hair with curtain bangs

Long layered hair with curtain bangs is a timeless choice that adds a sense of luxury and fullness to your overall appearance. By incorporating long layers throughout the back and sides, you create a weightless feel that prevents long hair from looking flat or lifeless. The curtain bangs act as the perfect focal point, drawing attention to your cheekbones and eyes while blending seamlessly into the rest of the cut. This look is particularly flattering for those with round or square faces as it helps elongate the silhouette. To style this, use a large round brush to flip the bangs outward for that classic 70s inspired sweep.

WHY WE LOVE IT
Creates weightless height, quick morning styling time, and high visual volume.
STYLING CONSIDERATIONS
Requires regular maintenance trims every 6-8 weeks to keep shape sharp.
TECHNIQUE USED
Point-cut layers with soft texturizing through mid-lengths.
BEST FACE SHAPE
Oval, Heart, Square, Round
HAIR DENSITY
Fine to Medium Density
