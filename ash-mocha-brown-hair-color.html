1. Ash mocha brown for warm skin

Finding the right ash mocha brown for warm skin is all about creating a beautiful, intentional contrast. While ash is traditionally cool, a mocha base provides just enough neutral ground to keep the look from appearing washed out on warmer complexions. The smoky quality of the ash can actually help to temper very golden or olive skin tones, providing a modern and edgy look. It is often recommended to incorporate a few neutral mocha ribbons to bridge the gap between the cool ash and the warm skin. This combination results in a very trendy, “cool-girl” aesthetic that breaks traditional color rules in the best way possible. It is a bold choice that shows a high level of style confidence.

WHY WE LOVE IT
Sleek mirror shine, soft face framing, and anti-brass cool undertones.
STYLING CONSIDERATIONS
Occasional color gloss refresh needed every 8 weeks.
TECHNIQUE USED
Feathered layer shearing with deep ash permanent glaze.
