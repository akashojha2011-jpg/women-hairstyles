15+ Blunt Bob for Thick Straight Hair With Sleek Shape

Cindy Marcus
Senior Hairstylist
| PHOTOS UPDATED ON SEPTEMBER 12, 2026
Reviewed & Fact-Checked by Licensed Master Stylist Elena Vance

On thick, straight hair, the blunt bob shines with architectural precision and high-shine polish. Strategic internal weight removal removes bulk while keeping the bottom perimeter crisp and solid.

This sleek silhouette frames the jawline naturally, ensuring your hairstyle stays neat, swinging, and controlled all day long.

Below, discover 15+ chic blunt bob haircuts for thick straight hair.

1. Black blunt bob

The black blunt bob is a timeless and dramatic choice that looks exceptionally striking on thick, naturally straight hair. The dark color emphasizes the precision of the cut, making every sharp line and angle stand out with high contrast. Because dark hair often has a natural shine, the blunt ends look particularly healthy and dense, giving the appearance of extreme hair health. This look is often associated with a bold, sophisticated personality and requires very little styling to look “done.” To keep the color vibrant and the ends sharp, use a color-depositing conditioner and schedule regular maintenance. It is a classic “femme fatale” look that never goes out of style and looks expensive.

WHY WE LOVE IT
Creates optical hair density, sharp geometric silhouette, and chic high-fashion appeal.
STYLING CONSIDERATIONS
Requires regular 4-to-6 week perimeter trims to keep line crisp.
TECHNIQUE USED
Zero-degree horizontal shear cut with point-cut interior texturizing.
BEST FACE SHAPE
Universal
HAIR DENSITY
Fine to Medium Density
